## Capture acceptance

- Record the actual isolated game window and its generated voice channel; confirm a playable capture contains both the image and speech before a longer take.
- Frame both participants without posts or walls hiding their faces. Camera collision avoidance remains unfinished, so inspect the shot before recording.
- Retain event IDs, model/cost timings and save checkpoints in a companion evidence file. Keep credentials, private desktop content and developer consoles out of the video.
- Label real-time footage, time compression, illustrative diagrams and planned capabilities distinctly.
- Match the end of each scene to its native receipt or delivered-speech evidence. Do not claim lip sync or spatial voice; both remain unfinished.
- Keep the unedited capture and a captioned cut. Website/social publishing is a later explicit delivery step, not implied by creating local footage.

The game currently runs with native audio disabled for crash diagnosis, while generated voices use the overlay channel. Capturing that channel and listening to the resulting mix is a required video check. A playback acknowledgement alone does not establish recording quality.
