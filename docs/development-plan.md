# Living society development plan

Updated 25 October 2026 from Calvin's explicit direction. This plan preserves the full objective: a playable and watchable Whiterun economy and society, with autonomous residents, grounded speech, persistent consequences and an RTS-style observer. Items below are requirements and work in progress, not claims of completion.

## Accepted direction

- Make the town feel established and continuously inhabited. Existing neighbours should recognize each other; new arrivals need their own knowledge history. An explicit scenario background now supplies initial acquaintance without fake conversations or automatic friendship. [Implementation and live limits](situated-dialogue.md)
- Expand from a staged pair to concurrent independent lives: persistent resident plans, participant reservations, local encounters, resumable routines and overlapping work/travel/conversation. The [background protocol](concurrent-society.md) now reserves six travel slots and two conversation slots, with checkpoint-interleaving tests. One native traveller overlapped a three-turn voiced encounter; another route failed to acquire its package. Broader capacity, economic serialization and vanilla routine ownership remain unfinished architecture. More model calls alone do not remove these limits.
- Qualify encounter persistence across scheduling: a live free pair disappeared between opportunity observation and background travel. Recent proximity notices now preserve eligible approach options briefly, with expiry, spatial and personal-knowledge checks. The third trial consumed a notice and completed an encounter during travel, but the selected pair was already nearby: the extended-range branch and causal scheduling benefit remain unqualified. The previous two zero-overlap trials remain in the denominator. Next work includes persistent plans and resolving native package acquisition failures, alongside dialogue grounding and reply latency.

- Use cloud models through OpenRouter. Local inference is optional future comparison, not a prerequisite. Mercury 2.5 is the leading dialogue candidate, subject to actual adapter, grounding, latency and voice checks. Keep Jev for bounded decisions and deterministic code for rules, accounting and native execution.
- Ordinary simulation must not depend on Ysolda, Carlotta, Adrianne or any fixed named resident. NPCs can enter, leave or change. Select partners, sellers, employers and destinations from current population, capabilities, observations and commitments. Keep named demonstrations explicitly labelled as fixtures.
- Each resident has a changing personal view of the world. Global simulation knowledge must not leak into their decisions. Meeting a stranger does not reveal their name, wealth, motives, personality or private memories. Names and other information require a recorded source. Rumours and speech remain claims until independently verified. Familiarity, affinity and trust are separate.
- Model-driven choices should have meaningful variety: trade and production, employment, meals and rest, courtesy, refusal, quiet passage, introductions, ordinary conversation, disagreement and relationship development. Generate feasible options from state; keep effect validation deterministic. Do not claim a large number of meaningful behaviours merely by multiplying cosmetic variants.
- Build full RTS observer mode in Skyrim: elevated overview, pan, zoom, rotate, select and highlight residents, follow them, jump between events, and inspect needs, activity, relationships, resources and businesses. Preserve first-person play as another mode. Simulation-speed controls must respect native game execution and persistence.
- Show the society visually and audibly: nearby speakers facing one another, clearly attributed speech, visible interaction state, informative overlays, and eventually recorded example videos explaining conversations, economy and consequences.
- Keep README, the Pages journal, system diagrams and evidence current as implementation progresses.

## Delivery sequence and evidence

Calvin's expanded acceptance direction is to implement and test the integrated experience, not stop at this plan or a small demonstration. The full programme includes a changing population, capability-based businesses and connected production chains, work and wages, consumption and scarcity, personal beliefs and social memory, introductions and relationships, ordinary conversation and drama, cloud language and audible voices, political/policy controls, and an RTS/first-person interface. Implementations must coexist in one persistent game session. Test rules and failure paths locally, then verify real movement, exchanges, speech, camera interaction and save/resume in Skyrim. Preserve distinct claims for authored scenario assumptions, synthetic test evidence and actual game effects.

The latest scheduling work observes native scene/combat/sleep occupancy, keeps busy residents' needs pending, and allows unrelated work after a travel failure whose release and settlement are verified. A real four-event director run completed two voiced encounters and two meals; checkpoint 1093 restored all 16 compared fields. Native failure continuation and the new 15-second package acquisition limit still need live qualification. Reply gaps remain roughly 10–13 seconds. Persistent diverse daily plans, richer economics and social consequences remain required. [Behavior and evidence](scene-aware-scheduling.md)

## Current boundaries

Personal identity knowledge and capability-based food sellers are implemented, including changed-cast tests. Ordinary initialized-town conversation requests now offer opportunities to current eligible residents; the pre-census legacy pair remains a compatibility fixture. An actual Mercury/Jev/Kokoro encounter and all 14 save-restoration comparisons passed. RTS overview and native projections render, and browser camera controls work; injected in-game button/keyboard delivery remains unqualified. Enterprise registration is now separate from census and supports current custodians, variable wages, durations, stock targets and closure through the visual observer. Job choices and native execution carry the exact agreed terms. These policies, prices and the single recipe remain authored assumptions; upstream supply, autonomous formation, politics and first-person integration remain required. The quality lab exposes save-derived reliability, timings, economic breadth and relationship counts. Unknown realism, eye gaze, raycast and lip-sync measurements remain unscored. Two model-driven encounters completed with two-party native attendance and verified release; all 16 save fields restored. Reply dispatch still takes about 15.8 seconds in the two measured scenes, and a resident-departure meal attempt held the mixed run. Natural longer dialogue, spoken economic negotiations, deeper production, endogenous prices, drama mechanisms, political controls, recordings and full RTS/first-person interaction remain part of the active goal.
